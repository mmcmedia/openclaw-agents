## The Fundamental Lesson

**AI CAN:**
- Generate prose for scenes (7.5 → 8.2/10 with refinement)
- Follow explicit constraints (when given clear instructions)
- Produce multiple variants for human selection

**AI CANNOT:**
- Design story architecture
- Select which version is best
- Maintain narrative continuity across chapters
- Commit to a single narrative spine

**The Working Workflow (Confirmed):**
1. Human designs story beats
2. AI generates prose for approved beats (one chapter at a time)
3. Human selects/edits/assembles
4. Repeat for next chapter

## What Broke Multi-Chapter Generation

**Problem 1: Variant Accumulation**
- Pipeline generated 3-4 versions of each scene
- Database saved all variants (unique IDs, duplicate content)
- Assembly concatenated everything

**Problem 2: Location Revisits**
- Beat generator revisited same places (clocktower, workshop)
- Different scene numbers, same narrative events
- Willow: "Reads like 3-4 competing drafts"

**Problem 3: Automation Overreach**
- Tried to automate continuity (failed)
- Tried to automate selection (failed)
- AI can't replace editorial judgment

## The Real Solution

**Pipeline Role:** Raw material generator
**Human Role:** Curator and editor

**Successful Pattern:**
- Generate Chapter 1 options (4 scenes × 2-3 variants = 8-12 scenes)
- Human picks best version of each
- Human writes Chapter 2 outline
- Generate Chapter 2 options
- Repeat

This is how professional writers use AI: **brainstorming partner, not replacement.**
