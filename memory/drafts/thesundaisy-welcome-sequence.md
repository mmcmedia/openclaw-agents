## Email 2: My Story (Sends Day 2)

**Subject Line Options:**
- Why I started making gospel printables (it wasn't for the money)
- The real reason behind TheSunDaisy
- From overwhelmed mom to... still overwhelmed, but with prettier walls 😅

**Body:**

Hey friend,

Can I be honest with you for a minute?

When I first started TheSunDaisy, it wasn't because I had some grand business plan. It was because I was drowning.

Three kids. Homeschooling. A church calling. Come Follow Me every week. And this constant feeling that I wasn't doing enough to bring the gospel into our home.

Sound familiar?

One day, I was looking for a simple scripture print to hang in our living room. Something beautiful. Something that would remind us of what matters. And everything I found was either:
- Too expensive
- Not quite right
- Or just... not "us"

So I made my own.

And then I made another. And another.

My kids started noticing. "Mom, I like that one." My husband would pause and actually READ the scripture on the wall. Friends asked where I got them.

That's when I realized: maybe other families need this too.

**TheSunDaisy isn't about selling printables.** It's about making it EASY to invite the Spirit into your home — even when life is chaotic and you're just trying to survive until bedtime.

That's why I'm here. That's why I create.

And I'm so grateful you're on this journey with me.

Tomorrow I'll share my most popular design (and why I think it resonates with so many families).

With love,
McKinzie 🌻
