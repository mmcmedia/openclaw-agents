# Morning Interview Questions for McKinzie

**Purpose:** Understand how to be even more proactive and valuable

---

## 🎯 Work Patterns & Priorities

1. **What's your typical day look like?** (When do you work on what?)
   - Morning routine?
   - Deep work blocks vs. admin time?
   - When do you check Etsy? Pinterest? Email?

2. **What takes up the most time in your day?** (Where can I save you hours?)
   - Customer service?
   - Content creation?
   - Product research?
   - Admin/operations?

3. **What do you procrastinate on?** (I can handle those things overnight)
   - Data entry?
   - Research?
   - Organizing?
   - Reporting?

---

## 📊 Decision-Making

4. **What decisions feel hard or slow?** (Can I pre-analyze options?)
   - Which site to focus on?
   - What products to create?
   - Where to spend ad budget?
   - What content to prioritize?

5. **What information do you wish you had at your fingertips?**
   - Revenue dashboards?
   - Competitor intel?
   - Trend forecasts?
   - Performance comparisons?

6. **When you're planning your week, what do you look at?**
   - How do you decide priorities?
   - What signals tell you something needs attention?

---

## 🔧 Pain Points

7. **What feels clunky or manual in your workflow?** (Automation targets)
   - Anything you copy-paste between tools?
   - Any spreadsheets you manually update?
   - Any reports you build by hand?

8. **What makes you think "I wish I had time to..."?**
   - Competitive research?
   - Content planning?
   - A/B testing?
   - Learning new strategies?

9. **What causes you stress?** (How can I reduce it?)
   - Money/revenue uncertainty?
   - Time pressure?
   - Decision paralysis?
   - Tech frustrations?

---

## 💡 Opportunities

10. **What's working really well right now?** (How can I amplify it?)
    - TheSunDaisy success factors?
    - We Heart This FB growth?
    - Any content/products overperforming?

11. **What would you do if you had 2 more hours a day?**
    - More of what activity?
    - New projects?
    - Strategic planning?

12. **What would 10x your business?** (Big picture)
    - More traffic?
    - Better conversion?
    - More products?
    - New revenue streams?

---

## 🤖 Fitz-Specific

13. **What am I doing that's most valuable to you?**
    - Keep doing more of this

14. **What am I doing that's not helpful?**
    - Stop wasting time on this

15. **What would you like me to own completely?** (Full autonomy)
    - Research?
    - Monitoring?
    - Specific dashboards?
    - Daily/weekly reports?

16. **How do you prefer to receive information?**
    - Morning briefings?
    - Reports on demand?
    - Notifications/alerts?
    - Dashboards you check yourself?

---

## 📅 Rhythms

17. **What changes by day of week?**
    - Monday priorities?
    - Friday routines?
    - Weekend work?

18. **What's seasonal in your business?**
    - Holiday prep timelines?
    - Back-to-school?
    - Summer slump?

19. **What monthly/quarterly rhythms exist?**
    - Monthly reviews?
    - Quarterly planning?
    - Tax prep?

---

## 🎁 Dream Features

20. **If you could wave a magic wand, what tool/dashboard would exist tomorrow?**
    - Be specific - what would it show?
    - What decisions would it help with?
    - How often would you use it?

---

## Format

I'll ask these conversationally, not like a survey. The goal is to understand:
- Where your time goes
- Where friction exists
- What decisions are hard
- What information is missing
- How I can 10x my value to you

**Outcome:** A clearer picture of how to be the most valuable overnight employee you've ever had.
