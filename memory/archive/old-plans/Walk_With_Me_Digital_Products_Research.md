## KEY INSIGHTS

### Price Points
- **Budget:** $2.00-$3.99 (coloring pages, single prints)
- **Mid-range:** $4.00-$6.99 (most printables, small bundles)
- **Premium:** $7.00-$19.99 (large bundles, bulletin board kits)

### Best Performing Niches
1. **Printables bundles** - highest volume
2. **SVG/PNG for crafters** - strong performer
3. **Bulletin board kits** - higher price, dedicated audience
4. **Pass along cards** - missionary/church activity use
5. **Coloring pages** - low price, impulse buys

### Opportunities
- Set bundles (6+ images) command premium pricing ($12+)
- Activity-specific items (treat toppers, cards) have less competition
- Bulletin board kits priced at $9.99-$19.99 with less saturation
- SVG bundles attract crafter audience beyond LDS market
