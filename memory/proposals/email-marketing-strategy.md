## The Minimalist Approach

Instead of "email marketing," think: **"staying in touch with people who like me"**

### Frequency (Start Small)
- **Weekly** for engaged audiences (TheSunDaisy/LDS, Moms Make Cents)
- **Bi-weekly** for content sites (Hello Hayley, etc.)
- **Can always increase later**

### Format (Keep It Simple)
```
Subject: [Curiosity hook]

Hey [Name],

[1-2 sentences of connection/value]

[The thing you want them to see/do]

[Sign-off]
McKinzie
```

That's it. No fancy templates. No 12-part sequences. Just genuine connection.

## 🎯 Strategy by Audience

### 1. TheSunDaisy (LDS Audience) — HIGHEST PRIORITY

**Why:** These customers already bought from you. They're warm. They WANT to hear about new products.

**Email Types:**
1. **New Product Announcements** — "New CFM printables for [week]"
2. **Seasonal Prep** — "Easter is coming - here's what I made"
3. **Behind the Scenes** — "Why I created this design"
4. **Value Content** — "5-minute CFM activity for this week"

**Welcome Sequence (3 emails):**
1. Day 0: Thanks + free download + what to expect
2. Day 2: Your story (why you create LDS content)
3. Day 5: Your best-seller + coupon code

**Ongoing:** Weekly "CFM This Week" email (I can draft these!)

---

### 2. Moms Make Cents (Blogger Audience)

**Why:** They're learning to make money online. You've done it. They trust you.

**Email Types:**
1. **Income Reports** — Transparency builds trust
2. **Quick Tips** — "The plugin that doubled my page speed"
3. **Tool Recommendations** — (Affiliate opportunity)
4. **Case Studies** — "How I recovered from the Pinterest crash"

**Welcome Sequence (5 emails):**
1. Day 0: Free resource + your story
2. Day 2: Biggest mistake new bloggers make
3. Day 4: Your recommended tools (affiliate links)
4. Day 7: Quick win they can implement today
5. Day 10: Invitation to engage (reply, follow, etc.)

---

### 3. Content Sites (Hello Hayley, etc.)

**Why:** These readers came for content. Give them more of what they want.

**Email Types:**
1. **Best of Week** — Curated top posts
2. **Exclusive Content** — Things not on the blog
3. **Seasonal Roundups** — "10 Valentine's Day ideas"

**Approach:** Lower touch. Bi-weekly digest. Easy to batch.
