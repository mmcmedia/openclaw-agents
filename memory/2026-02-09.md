**The hypothesis that failed:**
"If we add quality loops during generation, we'll catch problems early and produce better stories."

**What actually happened:**
- Caught some over-explanation ✓

- Stories got shorter (not always good) ✗
- Scene repetition still happened ✗

**Creek's approach that works:**
"Trust the AI to tell a story naturally, then polish once at the end."

**Why it works:**

- Natural length emerges (not artificially constrained)
- ONE edit pass is enough (not multiple micro-edits)
- Cheaper and faster
- Result: Submit-ready
