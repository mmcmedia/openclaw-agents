# Mediavine CSV Download Skill

## When to Use
Downloading earnings/RPM data from Mediavine dashboard.

## Where to Click
In the **"Earnings & RPM" section** (NOT "Earnings Summary" at top).
Top-right corner of section header: two icons 🔗 (link) and ⬇️ (download).
Click the **DOWNLOAD ARROW (⬇️)**!
