## Benchmarks for Digital Products on Etsy

### Meta Ads

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| ROAS | 2.19 (median) | 4.52 for ASC |
| ROAS goal | 4.0+ | Sustainable for digital products |
| CPC | $0.70-$1.32 | Seasonal variation |
| CTR | ≥1.0% | <0.5% = problem |
| CPA | $23.74 median | Lower for digital (no COGS) |

### Google Shopping

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| ROAS | 3.68 |
| CPC | $0.50-$1.50 |
| CTR | 4.13% |

### Why Digital Products Are Different

**Advantages:**
- No COGS = higher margins
- Instant delivery = faster attribution
- No shipping = no shipping complaints
- Infinite inventory = no stockouts

**Challenges:**
- Higher competition (low barrier to entry)
- Copyright/IP risks
- Harder to differentiate
- Lower perceived value than physical

## Product Feed Optimization (Google Shopping)

### Critical for Success

> "Garbage in, garbage out" — Feed quality determines Shopping performance

### Title Formula
```
[Product Type] + [Theme/Scripture] + [Occasion] + [Size/Format]
```

**Examples:**
- "LDS Scripture Wall Art | Joshua 1:9 Be Strong | Young Women Gift | 8x10 Digital Print"
- "Temple Watercolor Print | LDS Home Decor | Wedding Gift | 16x20 Printable"

### Image Requirements

| Platform | Image Type | Background |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| Google Shopping | Product-focused | White or transparent |
| Google PMax | Lifestyle | In-context, lifestyle |
| Meta | Both | Mix of white + lifestyle |

### Custom Labels (for bid segmentation)

Segment products by:
- Margin tier (high/medium/low)
- Best sellers
- Seasonal items
- New arrivals

## Budget Pacing Strategy

### Month 1-2: Learning
- Expect higher CPA
- Heavy testing
- Don't optimize too early (need 50 conversions/ad set)

### Month 3-4: Optimization
- Kill underperformers (3x Kill Rule)
- Scale winners (20% budget increases)
- Tighten targeting

### Month 5-6: Scaling
- Increase budgets on proven campaigns
- Add secondary platforms
- Expand audience reach

## Quick Audit Checklist

- [ ] Meta Pixel installed on Etsy (via Pattern or offsite)

- [ ] CAPI (Conversions API) set up
- [ ] EMQ score ≥8.0
- [ ] 150+ creative assets ready
- [ ] Product catalog uploaded
- [ ] UTM parameters configured
- [ ] Attribution set to 7-day click / 1-day view

### Monthly Audit

- [ ] ROAS by campaign
- [ ] CTR trends (fatigue check)
- [ ] Frequency by ad set
- [ ] Creative refresh status
- [ ] Top/worst performers
- [ ] Budget utilization
- [ ] Audience overlap check

## The "3x Kill Rule" for Etsy

> Any ad group with CPA >3x target gets flagged for immediate pause

**Example:**
- Target CPA: $15
- Actual CPA: $50 (3.3x)
- Action: PAUSE immediately

This prevents throwing good money after bad creative/audience combinations.
